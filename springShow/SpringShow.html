CELEBRATING OUR 10TH ANNIVERSARY

at St. John's Church Centre

120 Redhill Road, Rowlands Castle, PO9 6DF

Saturday 28th March 2026

Competition Entries 10.00 - 12.00

Judging 12.00 - 14.00

Public Entry 14.00 - 16.30

Entries Close Wednesday 25th March 2026


Submit entry forms to:

Fiona Charlesworth, 1 Yardlea Close, Rowlands Castle

Cathy Tyrrell, 85A Finchdean Road, Rowlands Castle

Show Secretary Catherine Tyrrell


Rules of Entry

  1. 1. Entry is open to both members and non-members of Rowlands Castle Gardening Club.
  2. 2. Exhibitors may only enter one exhibit in each class.
  3. 3. Entry forms to be submitted by Wednesday 25th March.
  4. 4. The hall will be open to receive competition entries between 10a.m. and 12 noon
  5. No entries will be received after 12 noon. Judging between 12 noon and 2 p.m
  6. The show opens to the public at 2 p.m
  7. Exhibits to be collected after 16.30. Any exhibit remaining at 17.00 will be disposed of.
  8. 5. All plant and vegetable material must have been grown by the competitor.
  9. 6. Pot plants must have been in the competitor’s possession for at least 4 months
  10. 7. Competitors may use purchased plant material and sundry decorative materials in sections B and D – No plastic oasis please
  11. 8. The judge’s decision shall be final.
  12. 9. The club will take reasonable ordinary care of` the exhibits but cannot be held responsible in any way for damage or loss to persons or property.

Full details of the rules, schedule and entry forms will also be available under Cultural and Social - garden club section at rowlandscastle.com
