Rules of Entry

  1. Entry is open to both members and non-members of Rowlands Castle Gardening Club.
  2. Exhibitors may only enter one exhibit in each class.
  3. Entry forms to be submitted by 9pm on Wednesday 26th March.
  4. The hall will be open to receive competition entries between 10a.m. and 12 noon.
    No entries will be received after 12 noon. Judging between 12 noon and 2 p.m.
    The show opens to the public at 2 p.m.
    Exhibits to be collected after 16.30. Any exhibit remaining at 17.00 will be disposed of.
  5. All plant and vegetable material must have been grown by the competitor.
  6. Pot plants must have been in the competitor's possession for at least 4 months.
  7. Competitors may use purchased plant material and sundry decorative materials in sections B and D.
  8. The judge's decision shall be final.
  9. The club will take reasonable ordinary care of the exhibits but cannot be held responsible in any way for damage or loss to persons or property.
