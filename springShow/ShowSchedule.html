Schedule of Entries

Section A

(Note - Narcissus = daffodil)

  1. One stem single flowered Narcissus
  2. One stem double flowered Narcissus
  3. One stem white Narcissus
  4. One stem split corona Narcissus
  5. One stem any multiheaded Narcissus
  6. Three stems (tall) Narcissus any variety all different
  7. Three stems miniature/Cyclamineus/Tete-a-Tete Narcissus
  8. One stem Iris any variety
  9. One stem single flowered Tulip
  10. One stem double flowered Tulip
  11. Three stems Tulip, same or mixed varieties
  12. One stem Camellia
  13. Three stems Camellia same or mixed varieties
  14. One stem Magnolia
  15. Single specimen of any other flower you would like to display

    16. All the above to be displayed in bikini vases supplied by the club

  16. Wallflowers same or mixed varieties displayed in a jam jar
  17. Polyanthus, any cultivar, max 6 stems in a jam jar
  18. Pansies or Violas displayed in an egg cup
  19. Vase of mixed spring flowers in a jam jar
  20. Vase of spring blossom (tree or shrub), max six stems
  21. Vase of foliage to be displayed for effect
  22. Single hellebore heads floating in entrants own bowl
  23. Double hellebore heads floating in entrants own bowl
  24. One potted Primula (any cultivar to include polyanthus auricula or primrose)
  25. One potted Orchid any variety
  26. Any other potted flowering plant
  27. Any potted foliage house plant

Section B
Floral Art

  1. A floral arrangement to celebrate Jane Austin who was born on 16th December 1775. Maximum size 45cm x 45cm.
  2. An artistic arrangement of dried flowers, seed heads etc.

Section C

  1. Rhubarb 3 stems (to show a small base of leaf)
  2. Bowl of winter salad
  3. Three specimens of any one vegetable
  4. Bunch of herbs in a jam jar (predominantly foliage)

Section D

  1. A planted spring container (not hanging)

Section E
Children's Section

  1. A display of flower heads (can be floating or set in damp sand)
  2. A floral card for Mother's Day
  3. A Mother's Day posy

Members Only Section

Hyacinth growing competition - Please bring your plants to be displayed whatever the condition. Prize for best and worst exhibit.
