Welcome to Rowlands Castle Gardening Club!

We are a local club with about 80 members, based in Rowlands Castle, Hampshire, UK.

Activities

Talks by carefully selected speakers on a range of topics of interest to gardeners. These take place in St. John's Church Centre 7.30pm on the 4th Monday of each month from September to April (except December). Non-members are welcome at these meetings (£5 per head).

Spring Flower Show in late March or early April — open to all with free entry for exhibits and £1 on the door. A friendly show to brighten the early Spring! Includes a sale of perennial plants and tea, coffee and cakes.

We have visits to gardens of interest. In 2025 there are four visits planned (for members only).

An informal Garden Party in a member's garden.

We also participate in other gardening related activities such as the PeCAN tree scheme and local initiatives to enhance our environment.

Membership

Annual membership runs from January to December each year and costs £20 per head.
